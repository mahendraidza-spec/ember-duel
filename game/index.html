🔥 EMBER DUEL

A card duel against Nyx, the Cinder Witch

You are the Ember Mage. Nyx has challenged you to a duel of cards: play strikes, guards and mends from your hand, spend your mana wisely, and burn her 30 health to zero before she burns yours.

Four elements, eight cards, endless second-guessing: block when she is about to hit hard, heal when you are low, and save your Meteor Fall for the killing blow.

  • Each turn you gain mana (3, then +1 a turn, up to 10) and draw 2 cards.
  • Click a glowing card to play it. Its blue gem is its mana cost.
  • 🛡 Block soaks damage until your next turn, then fades.
  • Reduce Nyx to 0 health to win. Fall to 0 and the duel is hers.
  • Keys: 1–7 play a card, E ends your turn, R starts a new duel.
